The cross

From argument to gospel

Winning an argument is not the goal. Each challenge points to a real longing in the Gita that the gospel meets.

LongingWhere the Gita reachesHow the gospel answers
A God who lovesKrishna calls Arjuna dear and invites devotion (bhakti), Gita 18:65God is love in His own being and proves it at the cross, 1 John 4:8-10
Freedom from the pastEscape from the cycle (moksha) through knowledge, work or devotionForgiveness given, not earned, Ephesians 2:8-9

A model to follow

In Athens, Paul began with what his hearers already worshipped and quoted their own poets before pointing to the resurrection (Acts 17:22-31). Do the same: honor the questions the Gita asks, use its own verses, and move to the person of Christ.

Keep it fair

These four challenges are drawn from Christian apologetics and target specific schools, mostly Advaita. Hindu philosophers have answered them for centuries, and some of their replies (on original sin, or on whether an infinite past is possible) are debated among Christian thinkers too. Present the arguments as questions to explore together, and expect to learn from your friend's answers.
