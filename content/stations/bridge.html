The cross

From argument to gospel

Winning an argument is not the goal. Each challenge points to a real longing in the Hindu texts that the gospel meets.

A God who loves
Where the Hindu texts reach

Krishna calls Arjuna dear and invites devotion (bhakti) (Gita 18:65)

How the gospel answers

God is love in His own being, and proves it at the cross (1 John 4:8-10)

Freedom from the past
Where the Hindu texts reach

Escape from the cycle (moksha) through knowledge (Gita 4:37), work (Gita 3:19) or devotion (Gita 12:6-7)

How the gospel answers

Forgiveness given, not earned (Ephesians 2:8-9)

Justice that makes sense
Where the Hindu texts reach

Karma as a moral law of sowing and reaping: as a person acts, so he becomes (Brihadaranyaka Upanishad 4.4.5), down to the kind of birth he is given next (Gita 16:19)

How the gospel answers

Sowing and reaping are real (Galatians 6:7), but grace meets the debt (Romans 6:23)

God among us
Where the Hindu texts reach

Krishna descends age after age (Gita 4:7-8)

How the gospel answers

The Word became flesh once, in history (John 1:14), and rose (1 Corinthians 15:4)

Know the Bible first

In Athens, Paul began with what his hearers already knew and quoted one of their own poets before pointing to the resurrection (Acts 17:22-31). The lesson is the order, not the quoting: start where your friend is, then bring them to Christ. As the guide said at the start, your confidence rests on knowing the Bible, not on mastering the Gita.

Add from the Gita only what helps: a few verses your friend actually cares about, or simply ask them which ones matter most and listen. It is fine to say, "I do not know that verse well yet. Tell me about it."

Speak the truth in love

These four questions are not tricks. They show that the Hindu worldview cannot carry the weight of real love, real justice and real forgiveness. Ask them gently, listen well, and always bring the conversation back to "Jesus Christ, and him crucified" (1 Corinthians 2:2). Arguments open the door. Only the gospel saves (Romans 1:16).

If you recognize these longings

They are not a sign that every path leads to God. They are a sign that you were made for Him. Jesus said, "Come to me, all you who labor and are heavily burdened, and I will give you rest" (Matthew 11:28).

Christ above all

Every longing the Gita reaches for, to be loved, to be free, to see justice done, to have God near, finds its Yes in Jesus Christ. All the promises of God are Yes in Him (2 Corinthians 1:20).
