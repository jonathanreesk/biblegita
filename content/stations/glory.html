The glory to come

Worthy is the Lamb

The Bible does not end in an argument. It ends in a throne room, a crowd from every nation, and God living with His people. Read the last pages slowly. This is where the whole journey has been heading.

Every nation, tribe, people and language

Scene one

Who is worthy?

In a vision, the apostle John sees a scroll in God's right hand, sealed with seven seals, and a mighty angel calls out through heaven and earth:

"Who is worthy to open the scroll and break its seals?" (Revelation 5:2)

No one is able, in heaven, on earth or under it. John weeps. Then one of the elders says:

"Weep no more; behold, the Lion of the tribe of Judah, the Root of David, has conquered" (Revelation 5:5)
Scene two

The Lion is a Lamb

John turns to see the Lion, and sees "a Lamb standing, as though it had been slain" (Revelation 5:6). The conqueror won by giving Himself. He takes the scroll, and heaven breaks into a new song:

"Worthy are you to take the scroll and to open its seals, for you were slain, and by your blood you ransomed people for God from every tribe and language and people and nation" (Revelation 5:9)
Scene three

Worthy is the Lamb

Ten thousands of ten thousands of angels join in (Revelation 5:11), and then every created thing:

"Worthy is the Lamb who was slain, to receive power and wealth and wisdom and might and honor and glory and blessing!" (Revelation 5:12)

All of creation answers, and the elders fall down and worship (Revelation 5:13-14).

Scene four

A crowd no one can count

"a great multitude that no one could number, from every nation, from all tribes and peoples and languages, standing before the throne and before the Lamb" (Revelation 7:9)

They cry out, "Salvation belongs to our God who sits on the throne, and to the Lamb!" (Revelation 7:10). Every nation includes every Indian language, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Bengali, Marathi and hundreds more. This is the crowd we invite our friends to join, and God will gather worshippers from every one of these languages.

Scene five

God lives with His people

"Behold, the dwelling place of God is with man. He will dwell with them, and they will be his people, and God himself will be with them as their God. He will wipe away every tear from their eyes, and death shall be no more, neither shall there be mourning nor crying nor pain anymore" (Revelation 21:3-4)

There is no temple in the city, because the Lord God and the Lamb are its temple. The light of the city is the glory of God, and its lamp is the Lamb (Revelation 21:22-23).

Scene six

They will see His face

The tree of life, closed off at the beginning of the Bible, stands open by the river of the water of life, and its leaves are for "the healing of the nations" (Revelation 22:1-2).

"They will see his face, and his name will be on their foreheads. And night will be no more... and they will reign forever and ever" (Revelation 22:4-5)

This is what you were made for: to know God, and to see Him, with joy that has no end.

The last prayer of the Bible

"Amen. Come, Lord Jesus!" The last words of the Bible are this prayer and a blessing: "The grace of the Lord Jesus be with all. Amen." (Revelation 22:20-21)

And right before that prayer, an invitation to everyone who is reading: "The Spirit and the Bride say, 'Come.' And let the one who hears say, 'Come.' And let the one who is thirsty come; let the one who desires take the water of life without price" (Revelation 22:17).

If you want to respond

Maybe you have read this far and something in you says yes. The Bible says becoming a follower of Jesus is not earned by knowledge, ritual or many lives. It is a gift received:

  1. TurnTell God honestly that you have gone your own way, and turn from it. "Repent therefore, and turn back, that your sins may be blotted out" (Acts 3:19).
  2. TrustTrust that Jesus died to forgive your sin and rose again. "if you confess with your mouth that Jesus is Lord and believe in your heart that God raised him from the dead, you will be saved" (Romans 10:9).
  3. FollowRead the Gospel of Luke or John, pray in your own words, and find a Christian friend or church to walk with you.

Not ready? That is all right. Keep asking honest questions, read a Gospel for yourself, and talk with the friend who shared this with you. "You will seek me and find me, when you seek me with all your heart" (Jeremiah 29:13).

Christ above all

Jesus Christ is worthy. At His name every knee will bow, and every tongue will confess that He is Lord, to the glory of God the Father (Philippians 2:10-11). Come, receive the free gift of His life, and bring others with you. Glory to the Lamb forever.
