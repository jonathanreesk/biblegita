Made to know and be known

Whatever we grew up with, we all ask the same questions.

Is there someone behind all this? Does He know me? What am I here for?

Underneath them all: a longing to be loved by someone greater than ourselves.

The Bible says that longing is real

You were made for it. God is love, He made you to know Him, and He has come looking for you.

Three things the Bible says about you

  1. Loved before you do anything "We love him, because he first loved us" (1 John 4:19). He "will rejoice over you with joy" (Zephaniah 3:17).
  2. Made on purpose, women and men alike "In God's image he created him; male and female he created them" (Genesis 1:27), with one calling (Genesis 1:28). The risen Jesus sent a woman, Mary Magdalene, to tell the others (John 20:16-18).
  3. Made to know God, with joy "This is eternal life, that they should know you" (John 17:3). "In your presence there is fullness of joy" (Psalm 16:11).

"Though you have not seen him, you love him ... and rejoice with joy that is inexpressible and filled with glory" (1 Peter 1:8).

What stands in the way

The Bible does not describe this first as a broken rule, but as a broken relationship. And God did not wait for us to fix it. Jesus invites: "Come to me, all you who labor and are heavily burdened, and I will give you rest" (Matthew 11:28).

God's answer is a person

Jesus is God come to us (John 1:14). He died to forgive our sin and rose from the dead (1 Corinthians 15:3-4). He says, "I am the way, the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father, except through me" (John 14:6). We say it plainly. It is a large claim, and it is also an open door: the invitation is to everyone, from every background.

Three promises before you start.

  1. We say where we stand This guide was written by followers of Jesus. We believe the Bible is true, and we say so plainly.
  2. The same fair test for both The Hindu texts and the Bible face the same questions. We describe Hindu teaching accurately, and say plainly where we think it fails.
  3. Check everything You do not have to agree to keep reading. If something sounds unfair, keep asking. Honest questions are welcome.

This guide is written to help you understand your Hindu friends and share Christ with them. The questions are about whether ideas hold together, never about mocking a text or the people who love it.

  1. UnderstandListen until you can state their view in a way your friend would agree with.
  2. LocateFind the verse your friend has in mind. Look it up together; you do not need to know it by heart.
  3. TestWalk through the argument. Name the logical problem plainly.
  4. AnticipateKnow how a thoughtful Hindu would answer. If you cannot say what their best reply is, you have not yet understood their view.
  5. BridgeShow how the gospel meets the longing underneath the question.

The four questions in the middle of the guide also name the logical issue in plain words. You do not need to learn the names.

Hindu thought is not one system. Three main schools read the same texts differently, so ask which one your friend holds before you press a point.

  • AdvaitaAll is one
  • VishishtadvaitaGod is personal, and we are part of Him
  • DvaitaGod and souls are always distinct
You do not need to know the Gita

This guide is not asking you to learn the Gita or the Mahabharata. Know the Bible, and know why you believe it. Where the Hindu texts matter, this guide gives you the background you need, and the glossary and the verse cards are here to look things up.

Always be prepared to give an answer for the hope you have, with gentleness and respect. (1 Peter 3:15)

A good place to start

Alongside this guide, read one of the four Gospels for yourself, Luke or John, and see who Jesus is in His own words. When Nathanael doubted, his friend Philip did not argue. He said, "Come and see" (John 1:46).

Christ above all

The One who made you is the One who came for you. All things were created through Christ and for Christ (Colossians 1:16), so the joy you were made for is to know Him. Everything in this guide leads here.
