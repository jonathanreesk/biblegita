Hope and mission · The gospel

I go to prepare a place for you

John 14:1-6

On the night before the cross, Jesus told His disciples not to be troubled (John 14:1-3). In His Father's house are many rooms; He was going to prepare a place, and He would come back to take them to be with Him. Every part of the gospel is in that promise.

Why the resurrection matters

  • We worship a living God. Jesus' tomb is empty. He is not a teacher remembered but a Lord alive today (Luke 24:5-6).
  • Death is defeated. He holds the keys of death; death no longer has the last word (1 Cor 15:54-57).
  • It is checkable. If Christ is not raised, our faith is useless (1 Cor 15:14-17). Christianity stakes everything on one event in history.
  • It is a preview. His resurrection is the firstfruits of ours (1 Cor 15:20-23).

Watch for this misreading

Some Hindus hear "many rooms" as "many paths to God," and the King James wording "many mansions" is sometimes quoted for this. Read the whole passage together. Thomas asks how they can know the way, and Jesus answers that He is the way, the truth and the life, and no one comes to the Father except through Him (John 14:5-6). Jesus said, "I am the door. If anyone enters in by me, he will be saved" (John 10:9).

Christ above all

Jesus is alive. He is the firstborn from the dead, and He holds first place in everything (Colossians 1:18). Because He rose, death itself will be swallowed up in victory (1 Corinthians 15:54-57).
