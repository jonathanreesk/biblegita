Going deeper

How the texts were passed down

Hindu tradition divides scripture into Shruti ("what is heard": the Vedas, preserved orally with strict phonetic methods) and Smriti ("what is remembered": the epics, including the Gita, which grew through performance). The Bible was passed down through manuscript copying, then recognized as a closed canon.

What to draw out

The two traditions treat variation differently. For the epic, additions are enrichment. For the Bible, variants are errors to be corrected, because the claim is that God acted in history and the record of it matters. That is the apologist's real point: the question is not which text is older or longer, but which one claims to report events that can be checked.
