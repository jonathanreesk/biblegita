Before you begin

How to walk this journey

This guide sets two scriptures side by side, one question at a time. Take it at your own pace and bring your honest questions. Where the two books disagree, it says so plainly and treats both with respect, because Hindu thought is not one system: Advaita (non-dualism), Vishishtadvaita (qualified non-dualism) and Dvaita (dualism) answer these questions differently.

The challenges here are about the logic of a worldview, not about mocking a text or its readers.
