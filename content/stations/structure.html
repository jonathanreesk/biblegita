How the texts are built

The Gita is not a standalone book. It is 18 chapters inside Book 6 (Bhishma Parva) of the Mahabharata, a battlefield dialogue between Krishna and the warrior Arjuna. The Bible is a library of 66 books written across roughly 1,500 years, each standing on its own historical ground.

The Hebrew Bible's 24 books hold the same content as the Protestant Old Testament's 39; they are counted differently. Expect a Hindu friend to raise canon differences. The honest answer: the core (the Gospels, Paul, the Torah, the Prophets) is shared by every Christian tradition.
