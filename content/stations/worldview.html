The questions · Time and history

Two shapes of reality

Hindu tradition counts four ages (yugas) that decline from truth (Satya) to strife (Kali) and then begin again. Most Hindus say we now live in the last of them, the Kali Yuga, shown above as the darkest arc. Each arrow points the way the cycle turns. The Bible describes one line instead: creation, the fall, the coming of Christ, and a final renewal.

Why it matters: on a cycle, no single event is final, so an incarnation can recur (Gita 4:7-8). On a line, the cross and empty tomb happen once for all (Hebrews 9:26-28). Most of the challenges below grow out of this difference.

Christ above all

History is not a wheel. It has a center, and the center is Jesus Christ. He came once, died once, rose once (Hebrews 9:26-28), and He is the Alpha and the Omega, the beginning and the end (Revelation 22:13).
