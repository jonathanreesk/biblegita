Go and make disciples

This step is written for followers of Jesus, about how they walk with friends who are coming to Him. You are welcome to read it and see what following Jesus involves, or go on to the last step, "Worthy is the Lamb".

Matthew 28:18-20
Why this is good news, not pressure

Making disciples is not recruiting for a group. It is inviting others into what you have found: knowing God, being loved by Him, and living in His purposes with joy (John 15:11). It begins with listening, because each person is loved by God as they are, before they believe anything.

Jesus did not send us to win debates or collect decisions. He sent us to make disciples, baptize them and teach them to obey, with His authority and His presence (Matthew 28:19-20). The arguments in this guide can start a conversation. Discipleship means staying beside a person as they learn to follow Jesus.

A pathway for a Hindu friendWhat following Jesus looks like, step by step
  1. Befriend and prayEat together, honor their family, pray for them by name. Look for someone open and welcoming, a "person of peace" (Luke 10:5-7).
  2. Discover ScriptureRead Luke or John together. Ask: what does this show about God, about people, what will you do?
  3. Meet JesusPresent the cross and empty tomb. Invite repentance and faith, turning from idols to serve the living God (1 Thess 1:9).
  4. BaptismA public step of belonging to Christ. Prepare them for what it may cost them with family and under the law.
  5. Teach to obeyPrayer, the Word, fellowship, the Lord's Supper, and new habits at home (Acts 2:42).
  6. MultiplyHelp them reach their own household and train others who will teach others (Acts 16:31, 2 Tim 2:2).

What following Jesus can cost

For a Hindu, turning to Christ is rarely a private decision. It can touch family, festivals, name, caste and livelihood all at once. These cards are here so you can see what your friend may face, and walk with them through it with compassion and patience. Do not rush them, and do not leave them alone in it. Jesus Himself told people to count the cost before following Him (Luke 14:28), and He promised that anyone who leaves home or family for His sake will receive a hundred times more, a new family, even with persecutions (Mark 10:29-30).

We want to be honest with you. For a Hindu, following Jesus can touch family, festivals, name, caste and livelihood. Jesus Himself said to count the cost first (Luke 14:28). He also promised that anyone who leaves home or family for His sake will receive a hundred times more, a new family, even with persecutions (Mark 10:29-30), and His followers are called to stand with you through it, with patience and love.

Know the law where you serve

Maharashtra passed the Freedom of Religion Act in March 2026, joining other Indian states with similar laws. A person who intends to change religion must give the authorities 60 days' notice, and conversions involving force, fraud or inducement carry serious penalties. Before any baptism, make sure the new believer understands the requirement, take advice from a lawyer or your church leadership, and never offer money, jobs or help as a reason to believe. Faith must be freely chosen.
